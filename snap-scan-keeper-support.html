Snap & ScanKeeper

VisionKit-powered document scanner. Live on the App Store.

Snap & ScanKeeper is a focused document scanning app for iPhone and iPad: VisionKit edge detection, automatic perspective correction, multi-page support, PDF generation with timestamp filenames, and custom Photos album storage. Privacy-first — everything happens on-device, no network, no tracking. Four save modes (PDF only / images only / both / share-only). v1.1 approved Apr 6 2026.
