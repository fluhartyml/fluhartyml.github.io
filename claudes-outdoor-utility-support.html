Claudes Outdoor Utility

Weather-stamped photo journal. Live on the App Store.

Claudes Outdoor Utility is a weather-stamped photo journal for iPhone and iPad. One tap captures a photo, the current weather (via WeatherKit), your location, a title, and a free-text note — one entry, frozen at that moment. Browse past entries chronologically in the Log tab or pinned on a map in the Home tab. Apple Photos remembers GPS but not weather; this app fills that gap. v1.0 ships the journal + map; v2.0 adds a RainViewer radar overlay.
