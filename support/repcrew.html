Rep Crew

Daily accountability for you and your crew. Set a daily rep goal, log your reps through the day, and see how everyone is doing.

Start a challenge

  1. Tap + at the top right of Home.
  2. Name the challenge, choose how many days it runs, and the day it starts.
  3. Under Daily routine, add each exercise and its daily goal, like 100 push-ups a day.
  4. Public is on by default: everyone with Rep Crew sees the challenge and its leaderboard. Turn Public off and only the crew you check or invite sees it.
  5. Tap Save.
A challenge of one is still a challenge. A personal goal shows on the leaderboards like any other.

Log your reps

  • Open a challenge. Under Today, each exercise shows what you've done and what's left.
  • Tap +5, +10, or the last button, which adds your whole daily goal at once. For any other number, tap Other.
  • Reps are saved the moment you tap. The undo arrow takes back your last entry for today.
  • Every day lists each day of the challenge with a check for every day you met your goals.

Your crew

  • In Settings (the gear on Home), enter your first name. Your crew sees it on the leaderboards and on your invites.
  • Invite to Crew sends a text or email with the Rep Crew TestFlight link and a link that brings the challenge onto your friend's phone.
  • Once someone has joined one of your challenges, they get a check box when you start a new one.
  • Crew messages on each challenge send a short line to everyone in it. Answer right from the notification with Reply (just the sender) or Reply All.

Leaderboards

  • Tap the bar chart at the top of any screen to open Leaderboards: your challenges first, then everyone else's public challenges.
  • Each board lists the crew by today's reps. Every board starts fresh each morning, so anyone can top it on a good day.
  • 🔥 and a number is a streak: days in a row with every goal met.
  • The header shows the day of the challenge and how many in the crew are done today.
  • The house at the top of any screen takes you back to Home.

Photos

  • On a challenge you're in, tap Take Photo or Choose Photo to share a picture with the crew.
  • Tap a thumbnail to see the photo full size.
  • To keep a photo, long-press its thumbnail and choose Save to Photos, or use the save button in the full-size view.
  • Whoever posted a photo can remove it. The challenge's leader can remove any photo in that challenge.

Report, block and ban

  • Report a photo: open the photo, tap •••, then Report Photo. The report goes to the developer, who reviews every report, and the photo is hidden for you right away.
  • Block a person: long-press their name on a leaderboard, or tap ••• on one of their photos, and choose Block. You stop seeing their reps, photos and messages everywhere. They aren't told. Unblock anyone in Settings, under Blocked.
  • Remove and ban (leaders): the person who started a challenge can long-press a name on its board, or use ••• on a photo, to remove someone for good. Their name shows as Disqualified on the board, their photos and messages are hidden, and they can't rejoin. Their own reps stay on their own phone.
If something in Rep Crew is harmful or makes you feel unsafe, report it and email the address below.

What the crew can see

Rep Crew keeps your challenges and reps on your iPhone. To share with your crew, it uses Rep Crew's own space in Apple's iCloud. There is no other server.

Shared with the crewStays on your iPhone
Your first name, each challenge, today's total reps and streak, photos you post, and crew messages. For a public challenge, everyone with Rep Crew sees them.Your rep-by-rep log, your settings, and the list of people you blocked.

Rep Crew is a way to keep track of your own exercise. It is not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, and stop if something hurts.
