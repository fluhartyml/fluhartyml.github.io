Memory Aid LockBox

A private, Face ID–locked vault for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Memory Aid LockBox keeps the things you can't afford to lose — cards, account logins and codes, photos, notes, a journal, contacts, receipts, and appointments — behind Face ID, organized into folders built for each kind of item. Point the camera at a card or receipt and it reads the details for you, all on your device. Your vault stays on your device and in your own private iCloud — there is no separate server, and the developer cannot see your data.
