My Person CRM

Personal CRM for iPhone — people, interactions, calendar.

My Person CRM is a small private CRM for staying in touch with people you care about. Pick someone from your Contacts (via the system picker — the app never sees your full address book), log interactions (date + note), and optionally mirror them to Calendar. Everything stays in SwiftData on the device. No accounts, no sync, no third-party services.
