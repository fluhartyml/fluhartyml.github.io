CryoTunes Player

90's-retro-inspired iPhone music player. Live on the App Store.

CryoTunes Player is an ice-blue retro music player drawing visual cues from the Winamp / Napster / LimeWire era, with modern Apple MusicKit underneath. 80+ stations organized as Category → Decade → Year. LCD ticker with time, date, and local weather (WeatherKit). Shares the CryoKit data layer with Tally Matrix Clock. Requires an Apple Music subscription.
