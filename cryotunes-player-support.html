CryoTunes Player

A modern music player made for iPhone Duo, iPhone and iPad. Live on the App Store.

CryoTunes Player is a modern Apple Music player made for iPhone Duo, iPhone and iPad. Album art fills your screen, and your player and stations sit on cards in front of it. 80+ stations organized as Category → Decade → Year. Requires an Apple Music subscription.

What it does

  • Full-screen album art — the cover of the song that's playing fills the screen behind your controls.
  • Card position — on iPhone Duo and iPad, choose which corner holds the player and which holds the stations.
  • Stations — mood, decade, artist radio, focus frequencies, and a sound machine (rain, surf, thunderstorm, ASMR loops).
  • My Music — browse your own playlists, albums and artists, and search Apple Music from inside the app.
  • Shazam — identify a song playing around you and add it straight to your Apple Music library.
  • Sleep timer — 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or 4 hours.
  • Smart Dislike — skip a song to stop hearing it until you reset the station.
  • Save favorites — thumbs-up a song to save it to a per-station playlist in your Apple Music library.
  • Share — send the song that's playing to a friend.
  • AirPlay — play to your speakers and Apple TV.
  • Shortcuts — play a station, play or pause, skip, stop, set a sleep timer, or see what's playing — from Shortcuts, Spotlight, Control Center or the Action Button.
